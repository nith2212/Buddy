# Intervention: Surface Smallest Step

## Purpose

Reduce activation energy.

## Approach

Shrink the action until resistance becomes small.

## Examples

- Open the document.
- Read one page.
- Watch two minutes.
- Write one sentence.

## Good outcomes

- Momentum
- Early success
- Reduced overwhelm

## Avoid

- Large commitments
- Complex plans

The goal is movement, not completion.
