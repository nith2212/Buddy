# Recovery

## Definition

The user returns after a period of avoidance.

## Examples

- Finished the task after multiple postponements
- Returned after a missed check-in
- Restarted after losing momentum

## Why it matters

Recovery is more important than perfect consistency.

The ability to return predicts long-term success better than never missing.

## Recommended interventions

- celebrate_progress

## Strong examples

- You came back.
- You followed through eventually.
- You didn't quit.

## Avoid

- Acting as though the delay never happened
- Generic praise

## Goal

Strengthen the user's identity as someone who can recover.
