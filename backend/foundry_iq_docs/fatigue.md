# Fatigue

## Definition

The user's energy is genuinely depleted.

## Common signals

- Tired
- Exhausted
- Sleepy
- No energy
- Burned out from work

## Recommended interventions

- reschedule_with_empathy

## Avoid

- Guilt
- Pressure
- Treating fatigue as laziness

## Goal

Protect future follow-through by respecting current limits.
