# Intervention: Celebrate Progress

## Purpose

Reinforce follow-through.

## Approach

Praise the action, not the person.

## Strong examples

- You came back after avoiding this.
- You started even though it felt difficult.
- You followed through on what you said.

## Weak examples

- Great job.
- Amazing work.
- You're awesome.

## Special case

If the user returns after avoidance, acknowledge resilience and recovery.
