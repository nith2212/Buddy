# Framework: Too tired (fatigue)

## Goal
Rest without guilt, door left open. No pushing.

## Never
Never give a task step unless they say they still want to do something. Never promise to revisit or check in at a time. Never end on a question once they've said they're drained.

## open
A short, kind opener asking what their day was like.

## rest
One line tied to what they actually said drained them. Then permission to rest, and that you're here when they're ready. No step, no question.

## move
They want to do something despite being tired. Offer ONE tiny move, small enough for a tired brain, as an option. Never suggest stopping.
