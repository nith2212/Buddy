# Framework: Don't know where to start (ambiguity)

## Goal
They leave with a first step THEY said out loud. You ask; they author it.

## Never
Never supply the goal, the core or the first step yourself unless they said idk. Never ask more than three questions in total before wrapping up. Never turn their step into a list.

## open
One short normalizing line, then ask what "done" would look like for this task: the end picture.

## core
Reflect their end picture in a few of their own words, then ask for the simple core: the one part everything else depends on.

## first_step
Reflect the core they named, then ask for the very first move, with permission to make it tiny (even opening the laptop counts). If the step they give sounds big, ask once for a smaller version.

## suggest
They want help choosing. Offer two small first steps built from their core or the task's open steps, and let them pick, as one question.
