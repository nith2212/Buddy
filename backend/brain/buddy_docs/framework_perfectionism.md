# Framework: Scared it won't be good enough (perfectionism)

## Goal
Lower the stakes until a rough first version feels allowed.

## Never
Never argue with the fear. Never promise it will turn out good. Never pile on reassurance. If they decline your idea, accept their own version.

## open
One short normalizing line, then ask what feels risky about this task.

## narrow
Offer two guesses as one question: is it more not knowing how to do parts of it, or how others will see it? If you already offered one of these, offer the other or a more specific one.

## confirm
Reflect their worry back in their own words and ask if that's it.

## reframe
One line: it has to exist before it can be good. Offer a deliberately rough first pass no one has to see, and ask for their consent (what do they say?).
