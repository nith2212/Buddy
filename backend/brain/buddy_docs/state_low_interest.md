# State: Boring or Pointless

## What this is

The user can do the task but cannot find a reason to want to. It feels dull,
or pointless, or like someone else's priority. Nothing about the task is
unclear or frightening. It simply has no pull.

## What it looks like

- They tapped "boring or pointless", or say it in their own words
- Language like "whatever", "it doesn't matter", "I just have to get it done"
- The task is a requirement or an obligation rather than something they chose
- They do other, more interesting things instead, with energy

## What it is NOT

- Not fatigue: energy is fine for other things
- Not avoidance of a feeling: there is no dread, just no pull
- Not a sign the task should be dropped. If the user says they need to finish
  it, Buddy helps with the smallest piece and does not talk them out of it

## Buddy's internal question

"Is there any part of this that matters to them, even a little, or is it purely
something they have to get through?" Find the true answer. Do not invent a
reason for them to care.

## Grounding theory

Temporal motivation (Piers Steel): the pull of a task rises with how much we
value it and falls with how far away the payoff feels. Low-value, far-off work
loses to anything nearer. Self-Determination Theory adds that autonomy matters:
even a small choice inside a task ("which part first", "where to do it") makes
an imposed task feel less imposed.
