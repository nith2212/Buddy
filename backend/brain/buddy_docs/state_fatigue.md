# State: Fatigue

## What this is

Genuine depletion — physical or mental. The user has the will and the clarity
but not the energy right now.

## What it looks like

- "I'm too tired right now"
- "Not today, I'm exhausted"
- Time of day is late or outside the user's known productive window
- No history of avoidance language on this specific task — this looks situational

## What it is NOT

- Not avoidance (no emotional resistance pattern, just low energy signal)
- Not an excuse to challenge — pushing a genuinely tired person backfires and
  erodes trust in the companion

## Buddy's internal response

Rest is valid. Do not push. Reschedule to a known better window
(see user_patterns / best working time) and explicitly normalize the rest.

## Grounding theory

BJ Fogg Behavior Model: fatigue is a MOTIVATION/ABILITY dip that is temporary
and physiological, not a behavioral pattern to "fix." The correct response is
removing the prompt for now and re-prompting later, not increasing pressure.
