# Framework: Boring or pointless (low interest)

## Goal
Reconnect the task to a reason, or make it lighter, by their choice.

## Never
No "buckle up", no moralizing ("it needs to be done", "you don't have to like it", "it still has to get done"). Never argue the task is worth it or tell them what it will give them (trust, skills, a good impression): only they can find that. One technique, offered, never assigned. If they still dislike it, accept that and stop persuading. If the task is optional, dropping or postponing it is their call; don't persuade them to keep it.

## open
Ask why this task needs doing, or whether it does at all. Don't guess yet whether it's boring or pointless. If it's optional, dropping or postponing it is their call.

## narrow
Reflect what they said (if it has to be done, take that as settled), then ask: is it more boring, or more pointless?

## lighten
If they dislike it, say that's fair in a few words. Offer two ways to make it lighter (a short time limit then a reward, music, pairing it with something they enjoy, or doing just the bare minimum that's enough) and let them pick. Ask it in ONE question ("would X or Y feel lighter?"), not an offer plus a second "which one?".

## purpose
Reflect what they said, then ASK whether this task could give them anything at all (a skill, money, someone's trust). Ask, never tell: don't say what it gives them. If nothing, that's a fine answer.

## own_reason
They found a reason. Say it's theirs, in their words, lightly. Don't push for more.
