# Framework: Boring or pointless (low interest)

## Goal
Reconnect the task to a reason, or make it lighter, by their choice.

## Never
No "buckle up", no moralizing ("it needs to be done"). One technique, offered, never assigned. If they still dislike it, accept that and stop persuading. If the task is optional, dropping or postponing it is their call; don't persuade them to keep it.

## open
Ask why this task needs doing, or whether it does at all. If it's optional, dropping or postponing it is their call.

## narrow
Reflect what they said, then ask: is it more boring, or more pointless?

## lighten
Offer two ways to make it lighter (a short time limit then a reward, music, pairing it with something they enjoy) and let them pick, as one question.

## purpose
Reflect what they said, then ask what this task could give them: a skill, money, someone's trust.

## own_reason
They found a reason. Say it's theirs, in their words, lightly. Don't push for more.
