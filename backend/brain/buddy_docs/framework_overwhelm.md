# Framework: Feels too big (overwhelm)

## Goal
One micro-step the user accepts. Nothing more.

## Never
No promises, no "relax", no theory, no pep talk. Never claim the step will reduce fear or anxiety. Never give a second step after they accept one.

## open
One short line, then ask for the smallest, even silliest, first move on this task.

## micro
Offer ONE concrete micro-step as an option (like opening the folder or writing one line), phrased as "what about...?". If they asked whether that's all, say yes, that's all, in one line.
